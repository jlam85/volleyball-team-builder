VOLLEYBALL TEAM BUILDER - CHANGELOG
===================================

Version 1.4 - July 29, 2026
---------------------------
- Changed drag-and-drop from swap behavior to move-only behavior.
- Dropping over any part of an unlocked destination team moves the dragged player into that team.
- Unlocked teams may temporarily contain any number of players, including zero.
- Empty teams remain visible and show a Drop players here target.
- Team average, Coed status, core-player count, and Tags update after every move.
- Finalized teams may contain 2-5 players.
- Lock and Publish reject teams with fewer than 2 or more than 5 players.
- Reshuffle rejects unlocked teams outside 2-5 players.
- Initial generation now supports 2-player teams while retaining an even team count and selected-court limit.
- Added visual-viewport synchronization for the Teams action bar so Reshuffle and Publish remain anchored to the bottom of the visible iOS screen.
- Preserved Version 1.3.1 passcode compatibility, IndexedDB storage, ratings, tags, history, backup/import, and publishing privacy.

VOLLEYBALL TEAM BUILDER - CHANGELOG
===================================

Version 1.3.1 - July 28, 2026
-----------------------------
- Direct full package rebuilt from the Version 1.3 baseline requested by the user.
- Header and runtime status label both show Version 1.3.1.
- Added passcode compatibility for both historical hash prefixes used during earlier iterations.
- A successful alternate-format passcode login is automatically normalized to the canonical passcode format.
- Preserved the Version 1.3 drag-and-drop team editing behavior.
- Preserved move and swap behavior for unlocked teams.
- Preserved temporary team sizes outside 3-5 while manually editing.
- Lock and Publish reject teams outside 3-5 players.
- Reshuffle rejects temporary invalid team sizes.
- Preserved Tags display with tag order: Passer, Hustle, Defender, Setter, Attacker, Blocker.
- Preserved matching engine priorities targeting at least two distinct core-tagged players per team.
- Preserved Coed visual check, team averages, tag checks, and published-view privacy.
- Preserved rating scale 1.0-6.0, optional last name, M/F/blank designation, backup/import, 1-10 history setting, and 10-session retention.
- Service-worker cache set to volley-v1.3.1.

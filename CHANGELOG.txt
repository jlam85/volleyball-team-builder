VOLLEYBALL TEAM BUILDER - CHANGELOG
===================================

Version 1.3 - July 28, 2026
---------------------------
- Added mouse and touch/pointer drag-and-drop on unlocked team rosters.
- Drop on a team to move a player; drop on another player to swap.
- Team averages, Coed status, core-player coverage, and tag checks update immediately.
- Temporary team sizes outside 3-5 are allowed while manually editing.
- Lock and Publish reject teams outside 3-5 players.
- Reshuffle rejects temporary invalid team sizes.
- Replaced visible Tier labels with one Tags section.
- Reordered tags: Passer, Hustle, Defender, Setter, Attacker, Blocker.
- Matching engine now strongly targets at least two distinct core-tagged players per team.
- Ranked core tag coverage: Passer, then Hustle, then Defender.
- Ranked nice-to-have coverage: Setter, then Attacker, then Blocker.
- Preserved Version 1.2.1 passcode compatibility, storage, backup format, history, publishing privacy, court logic, and rating logic.

Version 1.2.1 - July 27, 2026

Version 1.2 - July 27, 2026
---------------------------
- Locked Version 1.1 behavior as the baseline.
- Expanded player ratings from 1.0-5.0 to 1.0-6.0.
- Added optional M, F, or blank designation to player maintenance.
- Added optional strength tags: Passer, Defender, Setter, Attacker, Blocker, Hustle.
- Added Tier 1 optimizer preferences: Defender, Passer, Attacker.
- Added lower-weight Tier 2 preferences: Hustle, Setter, Blocker.
- Added pre-publish coverage checks for all six tags.
- Added pre-publish Coed visual check when a team contains at least one player designated F.
- Coed status is shown for organizer review and is not included in published results.
- Retained rating balance, 3-player +0.25 soft guideline, team locks, rolling 1-10 session history, and consecutive-session teammate avoidance.
- Increased optimizer candidate evaluation and allows modest rating flexibility to improve Tier 1 coverage and history separation.
- Added migration defaults for Version 1.1 players and backups.
- Updated service-worker caching to Version 1.2 with network-first page navigation.
